About

A Legal Professional Combining Academic Depth with Practical Experience

Dr. Jasdeep Kaur is a legal professional, educator, researcher, and practicing advocate based primarily in New Delhi, India. With over 11 years of experience in the legal domain, she has developed experience across family law, civil litigation, corporate legislation, criminal defence, and legal and taxation consultation.

Her academic background and research in family law, together with her experience in legal practice and public service, inform her approach to legal matters. She has served as a Law Officer for the Government of the National Capital Territory of Delhi and has contributed to child welfare initiatives.

Legal advice is only as sound as the understanding of the facts behind it — which is why research, careful listening and clear communication sit at the centre of the work.

  • Practical legal experience across family, civil, criminal, corporate and taxation matters, alongside court practice in Delhi.
  • Research-driven legal understanding — doctoral study and published research inform how matters are analysed.
  • Sensitive handling of family and child-related matters, with attention to confidentiality and the welfare of those affected.
  • Experience with child welfare matters and related public service work.
  • Commitment to professional ethics and client communication — clear expectations and honest assessment of a matter.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of legal matters can I enquire about?

Enquiries are welcome regarding family law, civil litigation and property disputes, criminal defence, corporate and commercial law, matters involving domestic violence and women's legal rights, and child welfare and juvenile justice.

Whether a particular matter can be taken up depends on its facts, the applicable law and professional engagement.

How can I request a consultation?

You may submit the enquiry form in the contact section, call or send a WhatsApp message on the number listed there, or write to the professional email address given. Please share a brief outline of the matter and the best way to reach you.

A consultation is arranged only after the enquiry has been reviewed and terms of engagement have been agreed.

What documents should I prepare before a consultation?

It helps to have a short written summary of events in date order, and copies of any relevant documents such as agreements, notices, court papers, correspondence, identification and, where relevant, financial records.

Please do not send original documents or confidential material in an initial enquiry. Bring or share them only once an engagement has been confirmed.

Does contacting the advocate establish an advocate–client relationship?

No. Sending an enquiry through this website, by email or by message does not by itself create an advocate–client relationship. Such a relationship arises only when the matter has been accepted and the terms of engagement have been agreed.

Until then, information shared is not protected by professional privilege in the same way as information provided under a confirmed engagement.

Can I enquire about family law or child custody matters?

Yes. Family law — including matrimonial disputes, custody and visitation, maintenance, and domestic violence matters — is one of the areas in which experience has been developed, supported by doctoral research in family law.

Matters involving children are handled with attention to their welfare and to confidentiality. No particular outcome can be assured in advance.

Does the website provide legal advice?

No. The content of this website is general professional information only and is not legal advice. Legal advice depends on the specific facts of a matter and the law applicable to it.

Please do not rely on anything on this website as a substitute for advice on your own circumstances.
